Frequently asked questions

Quick answers, when you need them.

What is Pickering Casino Weekevents?

Pickering Casino Weekevents is an event companion for performances listed for The Arena at Pickering Casino Resort. It brings current Ticketmaster listings, artwork, dates, venue information, local reminders, seat planning, group voting, and personal photos into one iPhone and iPad app. It is not a ticket seller, venue operator, booking agent, or gambling service.

How do I find an event?

Open Events to view the latest listings. Filter by category, date, and ticket status, or combine filters. Pull down to refresh. Tap an event to see its artwork, local date and time, venue details, notes, sales status, and official Ticketmaster link when available.

How do I save or remove a favourite event?

The app does not include a separate Favourite Events list. You can keep track of an event with a local reminder, which can be disabled again from the same event activity or reminder controls. You can also attach a selected personal photo to an event in My Photos, then edit or delete it later.

Why can event details change?

Dates, times, artwork, descriptions, ticket status, age conditions, accessibility information, and venue instructions are controlled by Ticketmaster, the venue, or the organizer. A short local cache can delay an update. Refresh Events, then confirm final details on Ticketmaster and the official venue website before travelling or purchasing.

Does the app offer gambling or real-money gaming?

No. The app does not offer gambling, wagering, betting, casino games, prizes of monetary value, deposits, withdrawals, or real-money gaming. It does not process gaming transactions or connect to a gaming account.

Does the app require an account?

No. You can browse events and use personal planning tools without providing a name, email address, password, or social identity. Ticket purchases open outside the app on Ticketmaster, which may require its own account and payment details under its own terms.

How is my information handled?

Selected photo copies and thumbnails, captions, seat plans, reminders, votes, preferences, and a short event cache stay in local app storage. The app has no advertising, behavioural tracking, or panlogo GmbH analytics service and does not ask for contacts, precise location, health, financial, or account information. Network providers may process technical request information when event data or artwork loads. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

How can I report incorrect event information?

Refresh Events and compare the listing with Ticketmaster and the official venue website. If it still appears incorrect, email support with the event title, displayed date and time, Ticketmaster link, the incorrect detail, and when you noticed it. A non-sensitive screenshot can help. Only Ticketmaster, the venue, or the organizer can change the official record.

How can I request privacy assistance?

Email the app support contact with “Privacy Request” as the subject and include the app name and App Store ID 6809417783. Most personal app content exists only on your device. You can remove local items in the app and manage notifications in iOS Settings; deleting the app removes its local container, although Apple backups may retain an older copy.

What information should I include in a support request?

Include your installed app version, iOS or iPadOS version, device model, the affected tab, steps to reproduce, expected and actual results, and your time zone for timing issues. For an event, add its title, displayed date, and Ticketmaster link. Never send passwords, ticket barcodes, payment data, API credentials, government identification, or screenshots containing private content.
