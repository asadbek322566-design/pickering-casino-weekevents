About the app

Made for moments worth planning around.

Pickering Casino Weekevents brings event discovery and personal planning tools together for performances listed at The Arena at Pickering Casino Resort.

Purpose

A clearer view of what is happening.

Pickering Casino Weekevents is an event-focused iPhone and iPad application created for Pickering Casino Resort. The application and its supporting website are developed by panlogo GmbH on behalf of Pickering Casino Resort, with Ontario Gaming GTA Limited Partnership identified in connection with the application. Current event listings are retrieved from the Ticketmaster Discovery API for the supported Pickering venue.

Discover and filter

Browse Ticketmaster listings, filter by category, date, and ticket status, refresh results, and open current event details.

Plan locally

Set optional local reminders, record a personal seat plan, compare two choices in a private vote, and organise selected photos without creating an account.

Plan with current information

Events evolve. Check before you go.

Dates, times, artwork, descriptions, ticket status, age conditions, accessibility information, and venue instructions may change. Refresh the app, then confirm final details and purchase conditions on Ticketmaster and the official venue website before travelling.

The app is an informational and personal planning tool. It is not a ticket seller, venue operator, booking agent, payment processor, or gambling service.
